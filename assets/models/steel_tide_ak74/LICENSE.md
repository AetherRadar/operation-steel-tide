The active first-person and world AK visuals are Blender adaptations of the
finished CC0 source model. The internal gameplay enum and saved-data key remain
`WeaponPlatform.AK74` for compatibility, while the player-facing weapon name is
AK-47 because the source silhouette, fixed wood stock, and curved 7.62-style
magazine identify it as that family.

The source `.blend` referenced four unpacked copies of two wood images and an
external HDR whose provenance was not supplied. The adaptation removes every
source image and material before constructing the runtime asset. None of those
missing files is distributed or reconstructed. The delivered 1024-pixel wood
base-color and roughness maps are deterministic, project-authored textures
generated by `scripts/blender/build_taradavies_ak47.py`; those new texture
pixels and the build script are covered by the repository's root MIT license.
The adapted firearm geometry retains the source CC0 dedication.

## DCC adaptation and runtime contract

The reproducible Blender 4.5 build rotates and normalizes the authored source
to the project's weapon frame, rebuilds six PBR materials, and delivers
separate first-person and world-detail exports. It separates the existing
authored magazine, charging handle, front sight, and rear sight into real
mechanism nodes. A compact optic bridge is refitted from the source model's own
rear-sight-base geometry; no primitive, CSG, or programmer-art rail is used.

Both GLBs expose the same named hierarchy, including `Magazine`,
`SpareMagazine`, `ChargingHandle`, `RearIronSight`, `FrontIronSight`,
`MuzzleDeviceTip`, `SuppressorTip`, `OpticRailContact`,
`OpticReticleAnchor`, and `EjectionPort`. The rail contact marker is derived
from a downward ray against the normalized adapter's actual top face. The
verified marker-to-rail gap is 0.000 mm in both exports, enabling the in-engine
micro, holographic, and magnified optics to enforce a +/-3 mm contact gate.

- First-person GLB: 97,372 unique source/export triangles, 11 mesh resources,
  six PBR materials, and two embedded PNG textures.
- World GLB: 24,488 unique source/export triangles, 11 mesh resources, six PBR
  materials, and two embedded PNG textures.
- Godot runtime counting includes the visible duplicate spare-magazine mesh:
  114,900 first-person and 28,870 world triangles.
- The authoritative `.blend` packs both project textures and stores only
  portable `//textures/...` paths.
