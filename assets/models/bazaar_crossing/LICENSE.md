# Bazaar Crossing Runtime Asset Rights

Every visible mesh embedded in `bazaar_crossing.glb` is an instance,
arrangement, or DCC adaptation of a whitelisted finished CC0 1.0 Universal
source. This includes the ground, route paving, site pads, three decks, six
stairs, fourteen guardrail runs, supports, fascia, Mid canopy, buildings,
cover clusters, and market dressing.

The structural source set is Trey Ramm/minime453's **Modular Industrial
Pieces**, published at https://opengameart.org/content/modular-industrial-kit
under CC0 1.0 Universal and acquired on 2026-08-27. The seven pinned modules
are:

- `IndStairsWideFull`;
- `IndFloorGreyPlatformFull`;
- `IndRoofTrimBStraightFull`;
- `IndColumnFree`;
- `IndColumnFreeCap`;
- `IndFoundationAStraightFull`;
- `IndRoofDarkGreyAngledFull`.

Their exact repository paths, palette object names, creator/source records, and
Bazaar mappings are listed in
`source_art/world/bazaar_crossing/LICENSE.md`. That file also records the CC0
buildings, props, and PBR texture sources embedded in this GLB.

Operation Steel Tide's MIT-covered contribution is limited to layout,
transforms and composition, material/UV adaptations, metadata, deterministic
build/validation code, and invisible gameplay scaffolding. Packing or adapting
CC0 meshes does not relicense those meshes as MIT. No generated box, prism,
CSG, or other programmatic mesh is shipped as visible Bazaar art.

This runtime asset contains no Hero Mountain, Coast Line, CC BY, paid,
editorial, private-store, or unclear-license source content. The full Jianghai
Old City GLB is not embedded or referenced at runtime.
