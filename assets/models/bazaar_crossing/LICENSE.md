# Bazaar Crossing V2 Runtime Asset Rights

Every visible mesh embedded in `bazaar_crossing.glb` is an arrangement,
instance, or real DCC adaptation of a whitelisted finished CC0 1.0 Universal
source. This includes the ground, interior floors/ceilings, complete building
walls and roofs, arcades, doors, windows, cornices, columns, counters, three
decks, six stairs, rails, three open approach forecourts, continuous wall
storage and shopfront bands, rooftop monitors/ridges, and limited market
dressing.

`B49CA049ABC5FD4E776877C8BCAEB5DC06379E1040AA19E49479436646A163D1`.
Its packed DCC source has SHA-256
`551BB6258752F088C3D75DA0DFA84B9459AD1C4E38A5C1C36DFA056352354EE1`,

The runtime mesh streams are not Draco-compressed; this packaging choice does
not alter the CC0 provenance recorded below.

transforms, composition, material/UV adaptations, metadata, deterministic
build/validation code, and invisible gameplay scaffolding. Adapting CC0 meshes
does not relicense them as MIT. No generated primitive, box, prism, CSG, or
other programmatic mesh ships as visible Bazaar art.

This asset contains no Hero Mountain, Coast Line, CC BY, paid, editorial,
private-store, or unclear-license source content. It neither embeds nor
references the full Jianghai Old City runtime GLB.
