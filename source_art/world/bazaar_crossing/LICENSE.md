# Bazaar Crossing DCC Rights and Source Record

Every visible mesh exported by the Bazaar Crossing DCC pipeline remains under
CC0 1.0 Universal and comes from an audited source object pinned in
`bazaar_crossing_source_palette.blend`. Packing, arranging, scaling, combining,
UV-retargeting, or material-retargeting those meshes does not relicense them as
MIT.

Operation Steel Tide's MIT-covered work is limited to the Bazaar layout,
transforms and composition, material/UV adaptations, metadata, deterministic
build and validation code, review cameras/lights, markers, and invisible
gameplay scaffolding. No programmatically generated box, prism, CSG, ground,
deck, stair, railing, support, or canopy mesh is presented as final visible art.

Attribution is not required by CC0; creator credit is retained as provenance.

The map-local palette is the authoritative daily-build input. Existing exact
acquisition filenames, hashes, and license evidence for the non-Trey CC0
content remain in the source-specific records already tracked under
`source_art/world/jianghai_old_city/`, `assets/models/`, and `assets/textures/`;
the Bazaar rebuild does not open that mutable Jianghai Blend.

Explicitly excluded: Hero Mountain, solararchitect assets, all CC BY content,
Coast Line 01, the Jianghai valley environment, the full Old City runtime GLB,
and any paid, private, marketplace-standard, editorial, or unclear-license raw
asset.
