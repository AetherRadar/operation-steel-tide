# First-Person Hands Art Brief

The 2026-09-21 direction is hands-first framing: show the selected operator's
hands/gloves and only a short length of wrist/forearm. Elbows, upper arms and
mesh end caps must remain outside the camera, including during reloads.

Use the approved operator Blender sources as the appearance reference. Do not
ship floating cut wrists or stretch character sleeves to compensate for a
camera mismatch. Author the first-person pose, skin, sockets and action clips
in Blender. Preserve the native SMG's animated magazine and charging-handle
interaction. Cover rifles, compact weapons, both pistol families, melee,
field medical use and climbing. Role changes must invalidate cached visuals.

The initial automatic operator-arm extraction was rejected during Godot review:
it exposed end caps or stretched forearm surfaces. It is not used by the game.
The existing first-person assets remain active. This brief is not a completion
claim; a reviewed DCC-authored replacement is still required.

Acceptance requires hand-to-grip and finger contact review at idle, ADS,
fire/recoil, every reload contact phase, melee and field-use poses; review both
16:9 and ultrawide framing. Retain screenshots and validate lifecycle changes
alongside the authored model and field-use diagnostics before delivery.
